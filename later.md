The intention of this application was to offer a way for people to practice tin whistle in an intuitive way similar to how I have seen there are apps that help people play piano. These apps generally show the notes of a song, and when you press the right key/note then it will keep playing and then when you do it incorrectly it will rewind or stop and wait for you to do it correctly.

I think we need to do a re-boot of the interface for this application. The midi and connections and settings in this regard to actually work with a real midi instrument works. But the interface needs to be more intuitive and newbie friendly. More so when it comes to the actual playing.

This is where we need to be inspired by established apps that does exactly what I want but for other instruments. I want song playback, perhaps even the real song? But somehow I guess we need to map the song to the notes for the software to even be aware of it. I do not know what the best way here is, having a midi that can be enhanced togeather with an mp3 so the song sounds real but the midi provides the notes?
Or is it better to crate songs for this song library using mp3 and then creating a tool to analyze the notes? But for best result it probably is unavoidable to do hands on tweaking. Perhaps we need to make a separate tool for this?

I think I also want notes to be rendered as well as the name of the note to learn notes in the periphery.
